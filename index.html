MODALS

Modal Auxiliary Verbs

Materi Kelas XI Semester 1

1. Pengertian (Definition)

Modals are auxiliary verbs used to express ability, permission, possibility, advice, obligation, prohibition, and requests.

Modals adalah kata kerja bantu yang digunakan untuk menyatakan kemampuan, izin, kemungkinan, saran, kewajiban, larangan, dan permintaan.

Contoh modal verbs: can, could, may, might, must, should, will, dan would.

2. Fungsi modals (Functions)

ModalFungsiExample
CanKemampuan / izinI can operate this machine.
CouldKemampuan lampau / permintaan sopanCould you help me?
MayMeminta izin / kemungkinanMay I enter the room?
MightKemungkinanIt might rain today.
MustKewajiban kuatStudents must wear safety equipment.
ShouldSaranYou should check the equipment.
WillMasa depan / kesediaanI will finish the task.
WouldPermintaan sopan / situasi hipotetisWould you open the door?
Have toKeharusanWorkers have to follow safety rules.
Must notLaranganYou must not touch live wires.

3. Pola kalimat (Sentence patterns)

A. Positive (kalimat positif)
Subject + Modal + Verb 1 + Object
  • I can repair the device.
  • Workers must wear helmets.
  • Students should read the instructions.
  • We will finish the project tomorrow.

Artinya: Saya bisa memperbaiki perangkat tersebut. Pekerja harus memakai helm. Siswa sebaiknya membaca petunjuk. Kami akan menyelesaikan proyek besok.

Important: Setelah modal, gunakan Verb 1 tanpa to.
Correct: She can operate the machine.
Incorrect: She can operates the machine.
B. Negative (kalimat negatif)
Subject + Modal + not + Verb 1
  • I cannot operate the machine.
  • You should not ignore the instructions.
  • Workers must not enter this area.
  • He will not come tomorrow.

Bentuk singkat yang umum: can’t, shouldn’t, mustn’t, dan won’t.

C. Interrogative (kalimat tanya)
Modal + Subject + Verb 1 + Object?
  • Can you repair this computer?
  • May I borrow your book?
  • Should we check the equipment?
  • Could you explain the procedure?

Jawaban singkat:

  • Yes, I can. / No, I can’t.
  • Yes, you may. / No, you may not.
  • Yes, we should. / No, we shouldn’t.

4. Penjelasan dan contoh

A. Can and could

Digunakan untuk menyatakan kemampuan atau meminta bantuan dengan sopan.

  • I can use a digital measuring instrument.
    Saya bisa menggunakan alat ukur digital.
  • He could swim when he was five.
    Dia bisa berenang ketika berusia lima tahun.
  • Could you help me with this project?
    Bisakah Anda membantu saya mengerjakan proyek ini?

B. May and might

Digunakan untuk meminta izin atau menyatakan kemungkinan.

  • May I use this computer?
    Bolehkah saya menggunakan komputer ini?
  • It may rain this afternoon.
    Mungkin akan turun hujan sore ini.
  • The machine might stop if it overheats.
    Mesin itu mungkin berhenti jika terlalu panas.

C. Must and have to

Digunakan untuk menyatakan kewajiban atau keharusan.

  • Students must follow laboratory safety rules.
    Siswa harus mengikuti aturan keselamatan laboratorium.
  • Workers have to wear protective equipment.
    Pekerja harus memakai alat pelindung.

D. Should

Digunakan untuk memberikan saran atau rekomendasi.

  • You should save your work regularly.
    Kamu sebaiknya menyimpan pekerjaan secara berkala.
  • We should recycle plastic waste.
    Kita sebaiknya mendaur ulang sampah plastik.
  • Technicians should inspect the equipment before use.
    Teknisi sebaiknya memeriksa peralatan sebelum digunakan.

E. Will and would

Will digunakan untuk menyatakan masa depan atau kesediaan.

  • I will complete the report tomorrow.
  • We will help you with the project.

Would dapat digunakan untuk permintaan yang lebih sopan.

  • Would you help me, please?
  • Would you explain this instruction again?

5. Perbedaan penting (Important differences)

Perhatikan perbedaan makna berikut karena sering muncul dalam soal Bahasa Inggris.

ExpressionMeaningExample
MustHarus; kewajiban kuatYou must wear a helmet.
Must notDilarangYou must not touch the wires.
ShouldSebaiknya; saranYou should check the wires.
Do not have toTidak harus; tidak wajibYou do not have to come early.
CanBisa; mampuShe can use the software.
MayBoleh; meminta izinMay I come in?
Perhatian: Must not berarti dilarang, sedangkan do not have to berarti tidak wajib. Keduanya tidak sama.

6. Contoh di lingkungan SMK

Electrical Engineering (TITL)

  • Students must switch off the power before inspection.
  • You must not touch exposed electrical wires.
  • Technicians should check the circuit carefully.

Geomatic Engineering (Geomatika)

  • Surveyors can use GPS to determine positions.
  • Students should record the measurement results accurately.
  • We must protect surveying equipment from damage.

HVAC Engineering (TPTU)

  • Technicians must follow safety procedures.
  • You should inspect the equipment before use.
  • Workers must not operate damaged equipment.

7. Kesimpulan (Summary)

  • Modals adalah kata kerja bantu yang menyatakan kemampuan, izin, kemungkinan, saran, kewajiban, atau permintaan.
  • Pola positif: Subject + Modal + Verb 1.
  • Pola negatif: Subject + Modal + not + Verb 1.
  • Pola tanya: Modal + Subject + Verb 1?
  • Setelah modal, kata kerja tidak menggunakan -s/-es dan umumnya tidak menggunakan to.
Remember: can = bisa, may = boleh/mungkin, must = harus, must not = dilarang, should = sebaiknya, dan will = akan.

Learning objectives

Students are able to:

  • Identify the functions of modal verbs.
  • Complete sentences using appropriate modals.
  • Use modals in daily and vocational situations.
